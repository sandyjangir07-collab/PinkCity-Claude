Diggi-Malpura Road, Jaipur.

Also searched as "Diggi-Bhilwara Road" — same corridor. Rajasthan State Highway 12 runs from Sanganer through Diggi towards both Malpura and, further on, Bhilwara district, so the road carries both names depending on which town locals reference. This page covers the single stretch nearest Jaipur.

Diggi-Malpura Road sits inside JDA's Master Plan 2047 zone, with genuine infrastructure drivers already in motion — Ring Road access and the Knowledge City (South) education hub at Chittora — but pricing that hasn't caught up yet. It's a real ground-floor opportunity, not a mature corridor: entry prices sit well below Ajmer Road and even below Shahpura. That's precisely the trade-off — lower risk than an unregulated periphery, but a longer runway to maturity than an established address.

Methodology: PinkCity's Investment Rating weighs five factors scored 1–5 by our advisory team: Connectivity & Infrastructure Pipeline (30%), Regulatory Clarity / JDA Approval Coverage (20%), Price Momentum & Entry Point (20%), Social & Commercial Infrastructure (20%), Employment & Demand Drivers (10%). Who Should Buy Here?
Long-Horizon Plot Investor
Comfortable holding 5–10 years while Ring Road access and Knowledge City South mature — the same setup that made Shahpura South Jaipur's top appreciator.
Budget First-Time Buyer
Priced out of Ajmer Road, Jagatpura, or Tonk Road but wants a JDA-planning-zone address with a defined regulatory framework.
NRI / Out-of-City Investor
A lower entry ticket inside a defined JDA framework rather than unapproved peripheral land — easier to underwrite from a distance.
Education-Hub Investor
Knowledge City (South) is designed to anchor institutional demand — worth watching for student-housing and long-term rental plot plays as it builds out.

Not the right fit for buyers who need immediate rental income or fully built social infrastructure today — that maturity is still a few years out, which is reflected in the price.
