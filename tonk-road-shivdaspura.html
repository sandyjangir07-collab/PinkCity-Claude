Tonk Road — Shivdaspura & Chaksu Belt.

This page covers the southern, earlier-stage stretch of Tonk Road — Shivdaspura and the Chaksu belt — not the already-established Jagatpura/Durgapura end nearer the city. Two confirmed, unusually strong catalysts set this belt apart: Rajasthan's first-ever JDA Land Pooling Scheme, and the newly Cabinet-approved Jaipur Metro Phase II.

This belt scores higher than most early-stage corridors because its catalysts are unusually concrete: JDA's first-ever Land Pooling Scheme under the 2016 Act is already sanctioned for Shivdaspura, Barkheda, and Chandlai villages, and Jaipur Metro Phase II — a 41 km, ₹13,037 crore, Union Cabinet-approved corridor — will serve Tonk Road directly. Neither is a "proposed, unconfirmed" rumor; both are documented government actions. What's still early is the social and commercial infrastructure around them, which is exactly why entry pricing remains low.

Infrastructure Tracker

Verified projects only — status and source shown for each.

Jaipur Metro Phase II (Prahladpura–Todi Mod)
✓ Cabinet Approved

A 41 km, 36-station North-South metro corridor approved by the Union Cabinet in April 2026, costing over ₹13,037 crore, with direct connectivity to Tonk Road, Sitapura Industrial Area, VKIA/Jaipur Airport, and SMS Hospital. Construction is pending; the approval itself is confirmed, not speculative.

Impact: HighSource: PMO / Union Cabinet, RMRCL
JDA's First Land Pooling Scheme
✓ JDA Sanctioned

Rajasthan's first-ever use of the Land Pooling Scheme Act, 2016 — roughly 163 hectares across Shivdaspura, Barkheda, and Chandlai villages, sanctioned by JDA and forwarded to the state government for final approval. Farmer land-share was raised to 47% to encourage participation, and the scheme includes EWS/LIG plots alongside planned roads, parks, and sewerage.

Impact: HighSource: JDA (jda.rajasthan.gov.in)
Ring Road — Tonk Road–Ajmer Road Link
● Operational

A 27 km section of Jaipur's Ring Road connecting Tonk Road and Ajmer Road has been complete and operational since March 2019, inaugurated by the Union Road Transport Minister.

Impact: HighSource: JDA / MoRTH
Jaipur International Airport (VKIA) Proximity
● Operational

Tonk Road runs past the airport belt, and Metro Phase II includes underground stations in the airport zone — a rare combination of existing air connectivity and future rail integration.

Impact: Medium–HighSource: AAI / RMRCL
Sitapura Industrial Area (RIICO)
● Operational

An established RIICO industrial belt along Tonk Road, and one of the anchor nodes Metro Phase II is specifically built to serve.

Impact: MediumSource: RIICO
Chaksu Bypass Road
○ Developer-Reported

Referenced by name in several project listings near Chaksu, but not independently confirmed against an official PWD/NHAI source as of this writing.

Impact: Medium if confirmedSource: Developer listings, unconfirmed
